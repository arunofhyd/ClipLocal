Stop paying annual subscriptions for clipboard apps that upload your data to the cloud.

ClipLocal gives you encrypted, lightning-fast clipboard history on your Mac with instant fuzzy search and zero cloud tracking. 100% on-device & free. 📋🔒

Install with one command:
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/arunofhyd/ClipLocal/main/install-cliplocal.command | bash
