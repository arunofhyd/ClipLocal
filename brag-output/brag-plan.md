# Brag Plan: ClipLocal

## What is this app?
ClipLocal is an ultra-fast, 100% on-device, encrypted clipboard history manager for macOS that remembers everything you copy without leaking your passwords or sensitive data to the cloud.

## The angle
Most popular Mac clipboard managers (like Paste) charge predatory annual subscriptions and upload your copied text, tokens, and personal messages to remote cloud servers. ClipLocal is the private alternative: encrypted SQLite database right on your SSD, instant fuzzy search, 100% native Swift performance, and zero cloud telemetry.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A scary cloud icon sniffing copied passwords and API keys.
Headline: *"Did you know your clipboard manager is syncing your passwords to the cloud?"*
Cut hard to: *"Keep your clipboard on your Mac. Meet ClipLocal."*

## Key moments (the middle)
- **100% On-Device Encryption:** Local SQLite storage protected by macOS security sandbox with zero analytics.
- **Lightning-Fast Fuzzy Search:** Global hotkey (`⌘ ⇧ V`) brings up a floating dark HUD with instant filtering across text, code, links, and snippets.
- **Direct Paste & Smart Clean-up:** Automatically strips formatting or paste directly into the active app in one keystroke.

## Outro / punchline
*"Your clipboard belongs to you."*
1-Command Terminal Install:
`curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/arunofhyd/ClipLocal/main/install-cliplocal.command | bash`
Subtext: `100% Native Swift · Zero Subscriptions · Free & Open Source`

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** User copies code, links, or text throughout their day.
2. **Action:** Hits `⌘ ⇧ V` → ClipLocal floating glass bar appears instantly with fuzzy search.
3. **Result:** Presses Enter → Snippet pasted directly into destination without leaving the keyboard.
